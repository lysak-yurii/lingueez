# Supported languages

Generated by `tools/gen_languages_md.py` — do not edit by hand.

- **Interface** — 32 languages the app itself is translated into.
- **Translate** — 105 languages you can add words and texts in.
- **Speech** — 65 of those have a text-to-speech voice.

Translation and speech come from different services with different coverage, so 40 languages can be translated but not spoken. The app greys out the pronounce button for those rather than failing on a click.
